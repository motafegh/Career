### C3-F01 — Charter's `reduced AI dependence over time` wording conflicts with accepted AI-era model

**AREA / OWNER:** `CAREER_CHARTER.md` §7  
**CLASS:** CONFLICT  
**SEVERITY:** HIGH  
**CONFIDENCE:** HIGH

**EVIDENCE**

The Charter's durable learning/project philosophy says Career should favor:

> `reduced AI dependence over time`.

The later accepted Strategy/Profile/P0–P7 model instead says:

```text
continued strong AI leverage
+ increasing verification / modification / diagnosis / responsibility ownership
```

and explicitly rejects AI avoidance/manual-only resilience.

**WHY IT MATTERS**

The Charter outranks Strategy. Read literally, this phrase can direct the Career system toward using **less AI as the objective**, even though the accepted strategy says AI use may remain high and what should decrease is uncritical/black-box dependence.

**P0–P7 RELATION**

Direct H1 confirmation. P0–P7 intentionally distinguishes reduced black-box dependence from reduced AI usage.

**PROVISIONAL DISPOSITION:** **CLARIFY / REPLACE wording only** — preserve the Charter's underlying ownership intent but express it as decreasing unverified/opaque dependence and increasing independent judgment/verification while retaining effective AI leverage.

**COUNTEREVIDENCE**

The phrase can be charitably interpreted as reduced *dependence*, not reduced *use*. Other Charter evidence-doctrine sections already reject AI-output-as-ownership. The defect is therefore semantic ambiguity in a high-authority sentence, not a broken Charter philosophy.

**NEXT EVIDENCE:** none material beyond final cross-artifact reconciliation.
