## 7. Access-gate checklist for Iran

For every serious Iranian vacancy, verify explicitly:

- current-open state;
- Tehran/other-city on-site requirement;
- ability/willingness to work physically in Iran for the role;
- degree and field;
- student/graduate status for internships;
- military-service requirement where specified;
- gender/age constraints where explicitly employer-stated;
- years of experience;
- prior software-company experience;
- language requirements;
- mission/travel/customer-site requirements;
- salary only after fit is established, unless it is itself a hard feasibility constraint.

Do not infer missing private facts from Career records.
