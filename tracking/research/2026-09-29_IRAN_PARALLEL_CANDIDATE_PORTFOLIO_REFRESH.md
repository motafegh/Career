## 4. Pool-management rule

The pool should normally contain **8–12 candidates with recent evidence**, distributed across several responsibility families rather than one title.

A candidate occupies an active slot only while it is:

- current/open or reasonably likely current pending immediate confirmation;
- plausibly within a current Career responsibility band;
- not already rejected on a hard access gate;
- worth the remaining detail-resolution cost.

Do not keep stale names merely to maintain a count.

### Preferred mix

Aim for a practical mix such as:

- 2–3 technical support / product diagnosis / implementation roles;
- 2–4 junior Python/backend/application roles;
- 1–2 internships/strongly mentored entry routes;
- 1–2 QA/evaluation or adjacent high-information roles;
- 1–2 AI-automation/applied-AI opportunities where actual entry expectations fit.

This is a **working search window**, not a quota or ranking.

## 5. Parallel application rule

Do not wait for one vacancy to finish its entire process before progressing another.

Allowed in parallel:

- prepare/submit one surviving candidate;
- resolve native duties for several others;
- refresh current/open state;
- refill slots lost to closure/access/rejection;
- collect interview/rejection feedback.

Do not:

- mass-apply with one generic CV;
- keep expanding beyond the bounded pool while many candidates remain unresolved;
- count a title-only/stale result as decision-ready;
- let application administration consume UpgradePilot's dominant cycle allocation.
