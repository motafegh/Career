# Career Security and Privacy Rules

**Owner:** Ali Rajabi  
**Status:** Approved and controlling public-safety policy  
**Responsibility:** Public/private boundary for profile, capability, portfolio, market, application, contact, and project-derived records

## 1. Public repository rule

This repository is public. Commit only information appropriate for permanent public disclosure.

A fact may be useful to private career reasoning and still be inappropriate for GitHub.

When uncertain, keep the detail private and record only the minimum generalized career consequence.

## 2. Never commit

- medication names, doses, schedules, prescriber details, medical records, treatment notes, symptoms, or private health history;
- private legal matters, police or court details, disputes, case identifiers, or legal correspondence;
- financial balances, debts, account details, payment information, or precise private financial circumstances;
- home address, private phone number, government identifier, passport, visa document, or unnecessary birth details;
- private family information or personal relationship details;
- passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys, seed phrases, cookies, session data, recovery codes, or credential files;
- `.env` files or unredacted local configuration;
- private SSH material or unredacted credential/host records;
- raw logs containing secrets, personal identifiers, private paths, or unrelated machine information;
- confidential job applications, assessment links, recruiter messages, offers, rejection correspondence, or employer documents;
- private contact lists, email addresses, phone numbers, referrals, or networking notes without explicit publication intent;
- salary negotiations, private compensation details, or confidential company information;
- private evaluator memory, hidden personal profiles, or sensitive conversation summaries;
- source material whose license, confidentiality, or terms do not permit republication.

## 3. Public-safe profile content

The public Career profile may include generalized professional facts such as:

- current employment status stated without unnecessary personal explanation;
- broad prior work experience and transferable responsibilities;
- public technical projects and repository links;
- public-safe technical exposure and assessed capability limits;
- career interests, working identity, and target role families;
- generalized time availability and workload mode;
- learning and work preferences;
- geographic and work-mode preferences at country or region level;
- public portfolio claims and limitations.

Do not include sensitive reasons behind a constraint. Record only the career-relevant consequence.

Examples:

- acceptable: “A reduced-capacity mode is active.”
- unacceptable: a medical explanation for reduced capacity.
- acceptable: “Currently not employed and focused on technical transition.”
- unacceptable: private family or financial detail explaining the transition.

## 4. Capability and project evidence

Before linking or recording project evidence:

1. verify that the source is public or authorized;
2. remove credentials and private identifiers;
3. preserve the minimum evidence required for the Career conclusion;
4. link to project-local technical evidence rather than copying large logs or work records;
5. distinguish Ali's work from AI-generated and AI-assisted work;
6. avoid publishing machine-specific paths, hostnames, IP addresses, fingerprints, or environment details unless necessary and safe;
7. respect licenses and avoid copying large third-party content;
8. state limitations and non-claims.

Career should normally record a career-level conclusion and link, not duplicate project source, tests, logs, or technical working memory.

## 5. Job-market and application records

Public market records may include:

- representative role titles;
- public job-description requirements summarized in Ali's own words;
- generalized gap patterns;
- public company and role links when useful;
- application-readiness decisions;
- anonymized aggregate feedback;
- public portfolio corrections.

Do not publish:

- private recruiter or interviewer identities;
- private email addresses or phone numbers;
- application portal tokens or assessment links;
- unannounced interview questions under confidentiality;
- confidential feedback or correspondence;
- personal notes about identifiable individuals;
- private compensation or offer details.

A future application tracker should be private by default unless each field is intentionally designed for public disclosure.

## 6. Contact and professional presence

Do not add personal contact details merely because resumes or portfolios often contain them.

Public professional links may be added when Ali explicitly approves the exact link and intended visibility. Keep private networking records outside this public repository.

## 7. AI and connected-source handling

AI assistants may use private context to improve reasoning when authorized, but they must not transfer that context into the public repository unless it is independently public-safe and necessary.

Before committing AI-generated content:

- remove sensitive inferred details;
- verify claims against evidence;
- avoid invented credentials, employers, education, dates, achievements, or metrics;
- preserve assistance disclosure when material;
- avoid copying private conversations or hidden memory.

## 8. Public claim standard

Public claims must be:

- specific;
- reproducible or inspectable where practical;
- evidence-backed;
- assistance-aware;
- explicit about scope and limitations;
- free of unsupported terms such as “expert,” “senior,” “production-ready,” or “enterprise-grade.”

Use “production-oriented” unless operational evidence supports a stronger statement.

## 9. Incident response

When sensitive information is committed:

1. stop further propagation;
2. determine whether the material is a credential, personal secret, confidential record, or unnecessary identifier;
3. revoke or rotate credentials immediately when applicable;
4. remove the content through the safest available repository process;
5. assess whether Git-history removal is necessary;
6. document only a public-safe incident conclusion;
7. strengthen the relevant prevention rule.

Never assume deleting the latest file version removes a secret from Git history.

## 10. Review rule

Review this policy before:

- creating or substantially expanding the public Career profile;
- adding application, interview, networking, compensation, or contact records;
- linking private or mixed-visibility evidence;
- publishing a resume or portfolio package;
- importing evidence from another project or service.

## 11. Maintenance

Change this policy only when the public/private boundary, evidence handling, market/application handling, contact policy, AI context handling, or incident response changes.
