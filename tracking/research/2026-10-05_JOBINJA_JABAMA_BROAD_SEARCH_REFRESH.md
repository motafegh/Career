## 4. Current search rule

Broader search does **not** mean weaker filtering.

Use:

```text
MORE KEYWORDS / MORE ROLE FAMILIES
→ more discovery candidates
→ current/native JD
→ access
→ responsibility fit
→ ownership stretch
→ application / hold / reject
```

The objective is to increase the number of plausible opportunities entering the top of the funnel while preserving strict evidence/fit checks at the bottom.
