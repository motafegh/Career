## 1. Vacancy shape

Basalam / SalamPay is hiring Product Builders / Problem Solvers across levels from internship through mid-level. The role is not conventional product administration. Employer-authored expectations combine:

- product discovery-to-delivery thinking;
- KPI/OKR reasoning and business/user alignment;
- technical literacy around Git, coding logic, APIs and system behavior;
- initial technical-feasibility judgment;
- translating product needs into clear technical tasks and specifications;
- root-cause analysis and structured problem decomposition;
- data-driven decision making;
- collaboration with engineering, UI/UX, data science and business;
- user-story / feature-spec documentation and stakeholder alignment;
- ownership, self-direction, learning and technical-English reading.

The direct employer page provides a live application form and does not state a minimum years-of-experience gate in the role description.
