## 1. Purpose

Apply the E3 four-way filter to a deliberately diverse first batch of current/current-looking vacancies.

This is not a mass-application list. It is an information-rich calibration set intended to distinguish:

```text
access eligibility
vs
responsibility fit
vs
Ali evidence/claim fit
vs
actual application decision
```

Career evidence is used conservatively. Private facts not recorded in Career are `UNRESOLVED`, not guessed.

## 2. Decision legend

### Access gate

- `PASS` — current Career/public evidence is sufficient to establish no known blocking gate.
- `FAIL` — a known gate conflicts with available evidence.
- `UNRESOLVED` — requires a private/user-specific fact or stronger current source.

### Responsibility fit

- `STRONG` — role shape closely matches approved narrow calibration families.
- `PLAUSIBLE` — meaningful overlap but less direct.
- `WEAK` — responsibility shape is currently a poor strategic match.

### Evidence / claim fit

- `DEFENSIBLE` — current Career evidence can support an honest application at the stated junior/bounded level.
- `STRETCH` — some required responsibility is materially weaker than the role asks, but a mentored/entry application may still provide useful calibration.
- `NOT DEFENSIBLE` — current evidence would require overclaiming or ignores a known central gap.

### Decision

- `APPLY IF ACCESS PASSES` — market/capability fit is sufficient; resolve private access/material gates then apply.
- `HOLD` — potentially relevant, but missing description/access facts prevent a defensible application decision.
- `REJECT FIRST BATCH` — current role/evidence mismatch would produce low-information noise.

6. Current Iranian platforms surface relevant Python, QA, support and ERP roles, but some direct-detail pages require native/manual inspection before a responsible APPLY/REJECT decision.
