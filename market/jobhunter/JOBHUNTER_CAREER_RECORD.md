## 1. Purpose

JobHunter exists to improve the quality of Career decisions by collecting, preserving, analyzing, and packaging public job-market evidence.

Its Career value is primarily instrumental:

- observe real vacancies;
- preserve source truth and provenance;
- extract responsibilities and requirements conservatively;
- compare recurring capability expectations;
- expose role/geography/experience constraints;
- support representative market calibration;
- provide evidence for Career review of gaps, targeting, and application readiness.

JobHunter does not decide Ali's career and does not automatically rewrite Career strategy, capability claims, portfolio allocation, CV claims, or application readiness.

## 5. What Career needs from JobHunter

Career needs trustworthy, reviewable outputs rather than technical feature count.

Priority outputs include:

1. representative current job samples;
2. visible source/search/geography/time-window scope;
3. de-duplicated distinct-job denominators;
4. responsibilities and requirements with evidence/provenance;
5. required/preferred/contextual and depth distinctions;
6. role-family and geography comparisons;
7. experience, education, collaboration, and work-mode constraints;
8. sample-size, concentration, source, translation, and model-quality limitations;
9. representative source examples and counterevidence;
10. Career-facing evidence packages that can be manually reviewed before decisions.
