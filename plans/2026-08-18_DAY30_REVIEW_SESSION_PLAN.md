```text

what the repository/system implements, tests, observes, or has learned
```

- repository sophistication does not raise capability automatically;
- AI-full-implementation work may create portfolio/product evidence without creating implementation ownership;

- reduced-context reconstruction, changed cases, failure diagnosis, meaningful challenge, test reasoning, and bounded modification are stronger evidence;
- recent moving-frontier work must not silently move the frozen August-18 technical target;
- uncertainty and explicit non-ownership are valid review outcomes;
- do not record private health, medication, contact, compensation, or application correspondence in the public Career repository.
