## 1. Purpose

Perform a rigorous, evidence-first audit of the Career repository itself after the September 2026 AI-era resilience research and the substantial evolution of Ali's project, capability, market, and portfolio strategy.

The audit asks:

> Does the Career operating system still express the right mission, authority structure, AI-era ownership model, project-selection rules, capability/evidence standards, market-feedback loop, workload/allocation logic, and agent procedures — without stale assumptions, conflicting owners, unnecessary ceremony, duplicated state, or rules that no longer earn their cost?

The goal is not to rewrite Career because newer ideas exist.

The goal is to determine accurately:

- what remains strong and should be preserved;
- what became stale as Ali/project/market reality changed;
- where P0–P7 was adopted incompletely or inconsistently;
- where older wording now points toward the wrong behavior;
- where multiple artifacts claim the same truth;
- where Skills/procedures are too heavy, ambiguous, duplicated, or insufficient;
- where Career may be drifting into project operation or failing to provide enough useful macro guidance;
- whether project-admission, learning, market, and capability rules still support Ali's actual remaining-cycle needs;
- what should be changed, narrowed, moved, merged, retired, or explicitly left alone.

## 2. Audit boundary and mutation rule

This audit is **Career-owned and read-only-first**.

During the audit:

- do not modify canonical Career governance, strategy, state, capability, market, portfolio, Skills, or active plans merely because a possible defect is noticed;
- preserve findings progressively in the dedicated audit report;
- distinguish `FACT`, `CONFLICT`, `STALE`, `GAP`, `COST`, and `JUDGMENT`;
- collect enough evidence to decide the smallest correction;
- finish the disposition matrix before remediation.

After the report:

- Ali decides whether to authorize a separate remediation pass;
- remediation should modify only the correct owners;
- historical evidence should remain history unless factual/link repair is required;
- `CAREER_STATE.md` should change only if the audit reveals a material live Career decision, not merely governance cleanup.

This plan does **not** authorize technical changes in UpgradePilot, JobHunter, Sentinel, or any other project.

## 6. Audit dimensions

Every material rule/artifact should be tested across the relevant dimensions below.

### D1 — Authority and single-owner integrity

Check:

- one normal owner per truth type;
- `CAREER_STATE.md` remains sole live-state owner;
- Strategy/Contract/Cycle/Portfolio/Market records do not silently compete;
- current plans do not redefine durable strategy;
- Skills guide procedure only;
- lower-level records do not overwrite higher-level contracts;
- project-local truths remain project-local.

Disposition concern:

> If two files disagree, can an agent identify the correct owner without interpretive guesswork?

### D2 — P0–P7 AI-era alignment

Check for consistency with the adopted model:

```text
use AI aggressively
+ preserve technical substrate
+ grow engineering responsibility ownership
+ emphasize verification / diagnosis / meaningful modification
+ distinguish transfer from project trivia
+ use filtered market feedback
+ reassess only from converging evidence
```

Flag wording that still implies:

- decreasing AI use is itself the objective;
- manual implementation volume is the core ownership measure;
- technical depth can be replaced by generic supervision;
- every AI capability jump is a strategy trigger;
- broad technology acquisition is resilience.

### D3 — Learning/capability strategy coherence

Check whether Career correctly specifies **what capability should become true** without taking over project-local learning execution.

Audit:

- Career outcome/evidence gates;
- project handoff;
- learning-by-building philosophy;
- responsibility-specific depth;
- changed-case/reduced-prompt/diagnosis evidence;
- when bounded standalone interventions are justified;
- whether `reduced AI dependence` language has become semantically stale relative to `increasing ownership with continued AI leverage`.

### D4 — Project portfolio/admission alignment

Check whether project-selection rules now support the new reality:

- one flagship;
- zero/one bounded supporting intervention;
- no technology hedge;
- no new project where a smaller lab/exercise/responsibility simulation is enough;
- admission based on real responsibility/market gap;
- active AI-full project assets represented consistently;
- historical-project reuse bounded honestly.

### D5 — Capability evidence and claim integrity

Check that the system rewards:

- mental model;
- verification/evaluation;
- meaningful modification;
- causal diagnosis;
- transfer;
- risk/accountability;
- technical explanation;
- appropriate agent/harness direction.

Check that it does **not** over-credit:

- AI-generated code;
- typing AI suggestions;
- repository size;
- passing tests alone;
- documentation sophistication;
- immediate repetition;
- generic prompt skill.

### D6 — Market-feedback and application alignment

Check:

- E2 is proportionate and does not become endless polishing;
- first applications do not require perfect ownership/SQL;
- application feedback is classified before changing learning;
- access gates change targeting rather than technical curriculum;
- high-information technical failures can justify bounded remediation;
- repeated market patterns can change role weighting;
- one employer-specific stack does not hijack strategy.

### D7 — Career↔project boundary quality

Stress-test whether the handoff model works:

```text
Career identifies outcome / evidence / allocation / reassessment condition
→ project decides technical HOW
→ project returns evidence at trigger
→ Career assesses
```

Look for both failure directions:

- Career taking over project planning/learning/source/test sequence;
- Career being so abstract that the project cannot understand what Career evidence/outcome is actually required.

### D8 — Skill routing and context engineering

Check:

- can a fresh agent choose the right Skill quickly?
- are no-skill cases clear?
- does `career-review` compose specialists only when necessary?
- does `career-personal-work-planning` stay above project execution?
- does `career-agent-retro` remain opt-in rather than automatic ceremony?
- are context reads proportional?
- do Skills duplicate root instructions excessively?
- can routing survive ambiguous requests and cross-project evidence questions?

### D9 — Staleness, supersession, and archive pressure

Check for:

- old project roles still stated as current;
- superseded review/application holds;
- stale 90-day/weekly/monthly priorities;
- obsolete identity/role wording;
- old plan artifacts that look controlling when they are historical;
- dead links/references;
- repeated old state embedded in durable files;
- missing explicit supersession when needed.

Historical truth may remain as history, but must not be easy to mistake for current authority.

### D10 — Proportionality and governance cost

For recurring Career process, ask:

```text
what decision / risk / evidence need does this process control?
is that need still real?
is this the simplest adequate mechanism?
what maintenance/context cost does it impose?
what breaks if narrowed or removed?
```

Audit especially:

- overlapping plans;
- repeated role lists;
- repeated live-status text;
- mandatory review ceremony;
- trackers/templates before activation;
- Skill over-routing;
- duplicated portfolio/market/capability conclusions;
- governance work that competes with real Career outcomes.

### D11 — Practical usefulness for Ali

A governance system can be logically correct but operationally poor.

Test whether it helps Ali answer quickly:

- What should I focus on now?
- Why does it matter?
- What project is primary?
- What kind of evidence am I trying to produce?
- When should I apply?
- What should I ignore/defer?
- What new project/supporting exercise is justified?
- When should AI news actually change my strategy?

If the system requires a long governance reconstruction for ordinary decisions, that is a usability defect even if every file is individually correct.

### D12 — Public safety and claim hygiene

Check that public files avoid:

- sensitive personal details;
- unsupported qualifications;
- inflated AI-assisted ownership;
- stale employability claims;
- misleading project activity/role descriptions.

## 10. Disposition vocabulary

Final recommendations should use only clear bounded dispositions:

- **KEEP** — correct and still earns its cost;
- **CLARIFY** — responsibility is right, wording/routing is ambiguous;
- **NARROW** — useful rule/process is broader/heavier than needed;
- **MOVE** — content belongs to another canonical owner;
- **MERGE** — duplicate owners should consolidate;
- **REMOVE** — no longer justified;
- **REPLACE** — responsibility remains but mechanism/model is materially wrong;
- **ARCHIVE / MARK HISTORICAL** — useful history but no longer active guidance;
- **ADD** — a genuinely missing durable responsibility exists;
- **NO CHANGE** — reviewed and explicitly found aligned.

No recommendation should exist solely because a file is old or long.

## 11. Audit severity standard

### CRITICAL

Could materially cause unsafe/privacy breach, false professional claims, major authority inversion, or a serious wrong Career/project action.

### HIGH

Could repeatedly misroute Career work, distort project allocation/learning/market strategy, or create materially false capability/portfolio decisions.

### MEDIUM

Creates recurring ambiguity, stale behavior, unnecessary process, or meaningful context cost but is unlikely to cause major harm alone.

### LOW

Localized clarity/navigation/maintenance issue.

### INFORMATIONAL

Observation or possible future improvement without present defect.

## 13. Audit principles

### Evidence before elegance

A shorter document is not automatically better and a more sophisticated governance system is not automatically better.

### Preserve working strengths

The current Career system already has valuable boundaries around project authority, claims, evidence, public safety, and anti-diversion. Do not damage them while chasing consistency.

### No P0–P7 cargo cult

Do not add `automation compression`, `transferability`, or other new terms everywhere. Adopt a concept only where that owner genuinely needs it.

### No governance for governance's sake

The purpose of this audit is to make Career **more useful and cheaper to operate**, not to generate more files and procedures.

### AI-era ownership without anti-AI ideology

If old wording can reasonably mean `less black-box dependence` rather than `use AI less`, clarify only if the ambiguity creates real behavioral risk.

### Project boundaries remain hard

Career may change what outcome/evidence it wants from a project. Career may not rewrite project technical governance during this audit.

### Current evidence beats historical prose

When current accepted strategy/state conflicts with an old plan/policy status line, determine the correct owner and repair the stale artifact rather than averaging the two.
