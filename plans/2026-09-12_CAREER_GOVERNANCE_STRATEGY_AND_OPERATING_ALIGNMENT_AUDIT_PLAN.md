## 1. Purpose

The audit tested whether the Career operating system still expressed the right mission, authority structure, AI-era ownership model, project-selection rules, capability/evidence standards, market-feedback loop, workload/allocation logic, and agent procedures without stale assumptions, conflicting owners, unnecessary ceremony, duplicated state, or rules that no longer earned their cost.

The objective was explicitly **not** to rewrite Career because newer ideas existed. It was to determine:

- what remained strong and should be preserved;
- what had become stale as Ali/project/market reality changed;
- where P0–P7 was adopted incompletely or inconsistently;
- where multiple artifacts claimed the same mutable truth;
- where Skills/procedures created ambiguity or unnecessary context cost;
- whether Career stayed above project technical execution while providing useful macro guidance;
- which content should be clarified, narrowed, moved, retired, or explicitly kept.

## 2. Audit boundary

The audit was Career-owned and **read-only-first**.

During C0–C12:

- canonical Career owners were not repaired merely because a possible defect appeared;
- material findings were preserved progressively in the audit report;
- findings were separated into fact/conflict/staleness/gap/cost/judgment classes;
- final dispositions were completed before remediation;
- project repositories remained outside the mutation boundary.

The audit did not authorize technical changes in UpgradePilot, JobHunter, Sentinel, or another project.

## 3. Freshness protocol

At audit start the process:

1. froze the inspected Career `main` horizon;
2. established the root authority chain and sole live-state owner;
3. inventoried governance/strategy/plan/portfolio/market/evidence/Skill surfaces;
4. recorded primary owner/blob evidence where useful.

At closure it checked whether `main` had advanced and reconciled only material deltas. The closure check found no parallel canonical Career change that invalidated the findings.

## 4. P0–P7 pressure-test lens

The audit treated P0–P7 as accepted evidence and a pressure-test lens, not terminology to propagate everywhere.

It tested for coherent representation of:

```text
responsibility/task analysis
```

## 6. Audit dimensions

Each relevant owner/process was tested for:

1. **Authority / single-owner integrity** — one normal owner per truth class; State as sole live action owner; Skills procedural only.
2. **AI-era alignment** — AI leverage plus technical/accountable ownership rather than anti-AI or manual-volume proxies.
3. **Learning/capability coherence** — Career specifies what must become true; project owns technical how.
4. **Portfolio/admission alignment** — one flagship, bounded support, no technology hedge, current roles separated from durable admission policy.
5. **Capability/claim integrity** — mental model, verification, meaningful modification, diagnosis, transfer, risk/accountability and explanation rewarded appropriately.
6. **Market/application alignment** — proportional E2, non-perfect activation, classified feedback, access-gate versus learning-gap separation.
7. **Career↔project boundary** — macro outcome/evidence/allocation from Career; technical continuation from project.
8. **Skill routing/context engineering** — clear triggers, no-Skill cases, lazy composition, proportional reads.
9. **Staleness/supersession** — historical artifacts unmistakably historical; mutable truth not duplicated into durable files.
10. **Governance cost/proportionality** — recurring process had to earn its decision/risk value.
11. **Practical usefulness** — Ali/future agents should recover current focus, reason, project, evidence goal and gates quickly.
12. **Public safety/claim hygiene** — no sensitive or inflated public claims.

## 7. Hypotheses tested

The audit explicitly tested whether:

- `reduced AI dependence` had become semantically stale;

- current role/application/gap lists were over-duplicated;
- old planning artifacts were too discoverable as pseudo-current owners;
- Skills still reflected pre-P0–P7 assumptions;

The final report records which hypotheses survived and which were rejected.

## 10. Final audit judgment

The audit concluded that the Career repository was **fundamentally sound** and did not justify:

- repository refoundation;
- a new governance architecture;
- a new meta-Skill/router;
- a replacement capability model;
- working-identity or flagship change.

The central maintenance problem was narrower:

> sound canonical decisions had been copied into too many mutable/current secondary surfaces; later reviews updated the owner while some copies remained stale.

The recommended repair was therefore subtractive and clarifying: restore single-owner mutable truth, historicalize completed plans, narrow stable Profile/Portfolio surfaces, preserve strong models, and remove technical-continuation duplication from Career planning.
