## 1. Purpose

The first employer-centric pass used Jobinja because its Top-50 and company-job pages are unusually inspectable.

This extension asks:

> What employer-level evidence can JobVision, IranTalent and e-estekhdam add without pretending their ranking systems mean the same thing as Jobinja's?

The objective is not to create one synthetic "best companies in Iran" score.

Different platforms expose different signals:

```text
Jobinja      → recent hiring activity + popularity + opportunity diversity + resume review
JobVision    → employee rating + resume responsiveness + interview experience + current company jobs
IranTalent   → current company openings + employee ratings + historical employer-brand evidence
E-estekhdam  → hiring-performance/company-identity ranking + industry/company discovery
```

These signals remain separate until a specific Career decision needs them.

## 3.3 Employer Branding Report — historical culture/attractiveness signal, not current hiring

IranTalent's 1403–1404 employer-branding report is a different evidence class.

The report says it analyzed evaluations from **more than 180,000 IranTalent users** accumulated over 17 years, while the main recent employer-brand ranking sections compare the **1399–1403** period against 1394–1398.

It evaluates companies through six dimensions:

- compensation/benefits;
- promotion opportunity;
- work-life balance;
- senior management;
- learning opportunity;
- culture/organizational values.

Important limitations stated by the report include:

- only companies with enough employee ratings can appear;
- small and some medium employers can therefore be absent;
- employer brand/satisfaction changes over time;
- annual updates are needed.

### Technology-sector historical signal

For 1399–1403, the report's IT/software/e-commerce employer-brand section includes companies such as:

- ArvanCloud;
- Snapp;
- Divar;
- Tapsi;

### Career use

Use this report only as a **historical employer-brand / culture / learning signal**.

Do not use it to say:

- these employers are currently hiring;

- their current roles fit Ali;

# 6. Employer evidence should now be part of every Iran vacancy decision

For a vacancy at a known employer, add this mini-layer before final application:

```text
VACANCY
current/open + native duties + access

EMPLOYER
current hiring activity
relevant-role recurrence
entry/junior history
resume-response/interview/employee signal where platform exposes it

ALI
claim/evidence fit

→ APPLY / HOLD / REJECT
```

Employer evidence can increase or decrease **information value and watch priority**.

It cannot rescue a vacancy whose hard requirements exceed current evidence or access.
