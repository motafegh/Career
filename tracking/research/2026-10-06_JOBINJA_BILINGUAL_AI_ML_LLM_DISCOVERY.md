## 5. Classification rule after discovery

Every AI/ML/LLM vacancy should be classified only after native-JD inspection.

### APPLY / strong candidate

Possible when the role is genuinely junior, trainee, intern, mentored or application/integration/evaluation oriented, for example:

- AI/ML internship;
- junior applied-AI implementation;
- LLM/RAG application integration;
- AI automation/internal tools;
- model/LLM evaluation;
- AI product support/technical integration;
- bounded NLP/CV work with mentoring.

### BOUNDED STRETCH

Use when some direct technical ownership is expected but the gap may be learnable inside a junior/mentored role.

### BENCHMARK

Use for clearly senior/specialist roles such as independent model research, senior ML systems, AI architecture/leadership, deep production MLOps, or multi-year specialist ownership.

Do not let benchmark roles dominate the application pool, but do keep them as market evidence.
