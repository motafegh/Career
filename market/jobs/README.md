# Serious Vacancy Records

**Status:** Active Career market record system  
**Purpose:** Preserve reusable public-safe history for vacancies that become important enough to justify their own durable record.

## Admission rule

Create one file in this directory when a vacancy reaches at least one of these states:

- **APPLICATION TRACK / APPLICATION READY**;
- **SERIOUS HIGH-PRIORITY or HIGH-STRETCH HOLD** with enough native-JD evidence that Career may realistically revisit it;
- **SCREEN / INTERVIEW / TAKE-HOME / OFFER** stage.

Do **not** create one file for every discovered vacancy, ordinary unresolved title, low-priority hold, rejection, benchmark, or stale listing. Those remain in dated market-refresh evidence under `../../tracking/research/`.

## What a job record owns

A job record is the durable public-safe lifecycle index for one vacancy. It should preserve:

- employer and role identity;
- source and last verified current/open evidence;
- current Career decision for that vacancy;
- concise responsibility/fit interpretation;
- important access or ownership-stretch boundary;
- links to claim audits, application packages, dated market research, and later public-safe feedback;
- status-history entries when the vacancy materially changes;
- the exact trigger for the next update.

It does **not** replace:

- `../EMPLOYABILITY_AND_MARKET_PLAN.md` for market/application policy;
- `../ALI_RAJABI_MASTER_CV.md` for canonical CV claims;
- `../../tracking/CAPABILITY_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md` for capability ownership;
- `../../CAREER_STATE.md` for overall live Career position and next Career action;
- vacancy-specific claim-audit files for detailed evidence reasoning;
- application-package files for employer-facing tailored copy.

Link instead of duplicating those artifacts.

## Privacy boundary

These records must remain public-safe. Do not store:

- phone, email, home address, or private location details;
- compensation discussions;
- recruiter/private correspondence;
- private eligibility or legal details;
- private interview questions/material unless explicitly safe to publish;
- private submission screenshots or account data.

Public-safe outcome summaries such as `submitted`, `screen`, `interview`, `rejected`, `withdrawn`, `stale`, or `offer` may be recorded when actually evidenced.

## Lifecycle states

Use the smallest accurate state:

```text
SERIOUS_HOLD
APPLICATION_TRACK
APPLICATION_READY
SUBMITTED
SCREEN
INTERVIEW
TAKE_HOME
OFFER
REJECTED
WITHDRAWN
STALE_OR_CLOSED
```

Do not mark `SUBMITTED` until actual submission is privately evidenced.

## Normal workflow

```text
dated discovery / market refresh
→ native-JD resolution
→ if admission threshold is crossed: create/update job record
→ claim audit when needed
→ tailored application package when applying
→ private submission / employer interaction
→ update only the public-safe lifecycle result here
→ aggregate meaningful feedback back into Career review when warranted
```

The goal is durable continuity without creating a large application tracker or company-dossier system.
