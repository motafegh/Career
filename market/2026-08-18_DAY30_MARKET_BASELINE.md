## 5. Day-30 market decision

### Role direction

Keep the current primary role families. Do not change working identity from this bounded sample.

Highest-probability near-term bands remain:

1. junior/graduate Python or backend roles with mentorship;
2. Python data/automation and junior data roles after SQL evidence improves;
3. applied-AI / AI-implementation graduate or junior roles where project evidence and assistance are explained honestly;
4. trainee/internship/structured-program routes when geography/eligibility permit.

### Capability priority

For the immediate correction window:

1. source/test/modification/debugging ownership inside UpgradePilot;
2. bounded SQL/relational-data evidence;
3. portfolio/CV/GitHub explanation and claim quality;
4. ordinary Git/code-review competence;
5. API/backend and deployment evidence when naturally reached.

Do not start a new advanced-system project to address the Day-30 market result.

### Application readiness

**Decision: not yet ready for normal calibration applications.**

The blocker is not lack of project sophistication. It is the mismatch between current market expectations for executable junior engineering work and Ali's still-unestablished current source/test/modification ownership, together with weak SQL evidence and incomplete market-facing positioning.

This is a bounded correction, not an indefinite hold.

Reassess application readiness at the earliest of:

- one meaningful UpgradePilot source modification plus representative test ownership and one real failure/diagnosis with reduced assistance;
- one bounded SQL/relational-data responsibility with inspectable queries/schema/change evidence;
- refreshed portfolio/CV/GitHub positioning sufficient to defend the three-project narrative;
- **2026-09-01**, whichever comes first with enough combined evidence.

A narrower internship/trainee/structured-program application may be considered earlier if an individual role has unusually strong fit and practical constraints are satisfied; that requires a vacancy-specific Career review rather than broad activation.

## 6. Sample limits

- This is a bounded current sample, not a statistically representative market census.
- Search and source availability can bias visible roles.
- Some public listings may close quickly or impose nationality, residency, degree, language, or location constraints.
- JobHunter's heterogeneous semantic validation is still in progress, so Career does not treat its current corpus as an authoritative aggregate market snapshot yet.
- Repeated market patterns should be refreshed before broader strategy changes or application activation.
