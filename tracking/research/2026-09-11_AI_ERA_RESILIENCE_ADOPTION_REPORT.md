## P0 — Evidence baseline and claim audit

**State:** NOT STARTED

### Trigger source

Anthropic Institute, *Scenarios for our Economic Future* / Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI, v1.0, September 2026.

### Initial bounded observations before full P0

- **SCENARIO/MODEL:** the model treats occupations as bundles of tasks that may remain unchanged, be augmented, be automated, or be replaced/expanded by new tasks.
- **SCENARIO/MODEL:** modeled outcomes depend materially on AI capability, adoption, autonomy, productivity effects, and worker adjustment/reallocation.
- **SCENARIO/MODEL:** Anthropic presents modest, substantial, and extreme scenarios rather than one forecast.
- **SCENARIO/MODEL:** the substantial scenario assumes AI can perform roughly half of knowledge work by 2030, with much of that capacity autonomous but incompletely adopted.
- **SCENARIO/MODEL:** the extreme scenario assumes AI is more productive than humans at the vast majority of knowledge-work tasks, does nearly all autonomously, and creates essentially no new human knowledge tasks.
- **SCENARIO/MODEL:** the model explicitly reports major limitations and open criticism, including disagreement about whether AI-exposed occupations necessarily shrink and whether the extreme case should be read primarily as a thought experiment.
- **JUDGMENT:** this is appropriate evidence for strategy pressure-testing, not direct proof that Ali's target occupations will collapse or that technical learning is obsolete.

### Current provisional consequence

Our existing strategy should be pressure-tested around **responsibility durability and transfer**, not replaced by an anti-technical or manual-only strategy.
