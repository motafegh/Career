## 5. Access-gate conclusion

The broad market sample repeatedly exposes gates independent of technical fit:

- degree / graduate-program status;
- local-language requirements, especially German;
- UAE-national or other nationality programs;
- work authorization / sponsorship constraints;
- location/on-site requirements;
- prior internships or one-to-three years of relevant experience.

Career's public-safe profile intentionally does not contain enough verified information to decide all of these globally.

Therefore every candidate vacancy must pass a private vacancy-specific access check. An otherwise strong role is **unresolved**, not automatically applicable, when an access gate is unknown.
