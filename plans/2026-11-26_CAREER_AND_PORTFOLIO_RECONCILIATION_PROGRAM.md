## 1. Why now

The October 18–November 17 conversion cycle expired. Career main had not incorporated the November 22 owner-authorized AegisLab evolution, newer UpgradePilot product/learning evidence, JobHunter semantic research, or Sentinel's resumed research. The September capability ledger and Master CV predate these developments. Old vacancy materials are reusable but their opening status is not current. Career must reconcile the actual portfolio without mistaking AI development output for personal mastery or allowing reconciliation to become another lengthy substitute for employment action.

## 2. Principles and acceptance

- Preserve the Career/project boundary; each technical repository remains authoritative for implementation and learning execution.
- Separate repository capability, personal direction, guided reasoning, direct execution/modification/diagnosis, delayed recall and transfer. No automatic D-level increase from test counts or documentation.
- Keep a single primary personal learning route by default. A technical project may continue AI-led without competing for personal attention. Compare AegisLab and UpgradePilot rather than assuming newer is better.
- Distinguish long-term specialization, present defensible identity, first employment and employer-facing headline. No unsupported seniority or narrow title gate.
- Prefer real operations and useful decisions to increasingly elaborate validation architecture alone. Every personal learning objective needs observable evidence and a stop condition.
- Preserve current safe workload ceiling of 24 focused hours/week; treat it as a ceiling, not quota. Expired allocation percentages are not renewed by default.
- Resume feasible current applications in parallel; no need to wait for the full program to finish. Private submissions, correspondence and practical access details remain private; never infer them from missing public records.
- No new project, major technical redesign, or skill-system rebuild is authorized merely by this program. Use existing Career skills and projects-learning where useful.
- Work in bounded reviews; target closeout or explicit re-scope by 2026-12-03, not an indefinite strategy phase. No calendar deadline forces false evidence.
