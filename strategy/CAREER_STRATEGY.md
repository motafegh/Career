**Owner:** Ali Rajabi  
**Status:** Approved and controlling Career strategy  
**Activated:** 2026-07-27  
**Responsibility:** Long-term direction, working identity, role targets, capability priorities, portfolio strategy, geography, market-entry logic, and strategy change criteria

## 1. Strategic objective

The objective is to move Ali from a non-technical employment history and mixed AI-assisted technical exposure into a credible first technical role supported by demonstrable capability, a defensible portfolio, and honest ownership evidence.

The strategy must optimize for:

- employability rather than technology collection;
- practical engineering responsibility rather than course completion;
- transferable capability rather than one-repository familiarity;
- visible product and evidence outcomes rather than documentation volume;
- effective AI-assisted work with increasing human verification, modification, diagnosis, and ownership;
- a path that remains ambitious without pretending current seniority.

## 2. Long-term direction

Ali's long-term direction is hybrid engineering across:

- Python software and automation;
- data acquisition, transformation, persistence, and analysis;
- machine learning and applied AI;
- secure and trustworthy engineering;
- selected backend, cloud, distributed, agentic, and MLOps responsibilities.

This is a direction of travel, not a requirement to establish professional depth in every area simultaneously.

## 3. Current working identity

> **AI-augmented Python/data/ML engineer developing secure engineering capability.**

This identity is intentionally narrower than the long-term ambition.

It means:

- Python and data work are the current implementation core;
- machine learning and applied AI are analytical specializations to be admitted through data and evaluation evidence;
- secure engineering is a product-quality and trust responsibility, not only a separate security role;
- AI is a work multiplier whose contribution must be disclosed;
- backend, cloud, distributed, agentic, and MLOps capabilities are developed when a project or role need justifies them.

Change the working identity only through a formal strategy review supported by capability and market evidence.

## 7. Capability-building strategy

The primary method is project-embedded learning:

```text
real responsibility
→ minimum mental model
→ Ali predicts or challenges
→ bounded action
→ actual output or failure
→ inspection and diagnosis
→ Ali modification, test, or explanation
→ evidence and assistance record
→ changed or reduced-prompt reassessment
```

Standalone study is justified when:

- the gap blocks safe action;
- the concept cannot be learned clearly within the active responsibility;
- repeated project failures show a broader foundation gap;
- a market-readiness assessment identifies a common missing foundation;
- a bounded credential or structured course has clear evidence value.

Study should return to an applied responsibility as soon as the required depth is reached.

## 8. AI-era engineering strategy

Ali should develop both:

- the ability to use AI assistants effectively for exploration, generation, review, and acceleration;
- the ability to verify, modify, test, diagnose, explain, and own important work.

The strategy rejects two extremes:

```text
AI avoidance
= ignoring a major modern engineering tool

AI substitution
= allowing generated work to replace capability and judgment
```

The desired capability is AI-augmented engineering with explicit human responsibility.

## 12. Market-entry strategy

Market work has four stages:

### M0 — orientation

- define target role families;
- collect representative role requirements;
- identify common evidence expectations;
- avoid applications until basic positioning exists.

### M1 — calibration

- map requirements to evidence and gaps;
- improve resume, GitHub, and project explanations;
- conduct small readiness checks;
- identify roles where current evidence is plausible.

### M2 — active applications

- apply to a bounded, representative set of roles;
- track outcomes privately or in a public-safe aggregate;
- prepare for interviews around actual role patterns;
- use feedback to correct claims and gaps.

### M3 — transition and iteration

- evaluate offers, rejections, interviews, and role fit;
- adjust strategy from patterns rather than one result;
- preserve the path toward the first technical role and later development.

Ali should not wait for every capability gap to close before M2. Application activation requires a reasoned readiness decision, not perfection.

## 13. Current strategic non-claims

The strategy does not claim that Ali is currently:

- mid-level or senior;
- independently job-ready for every role family;
- a professional security, ML, cloud, or distributed-systems engineer;
- production-ready because a project is production-oriented;
- capable of owning all prior AI-assisted project architecture;
- required to achieve advanced mastery before applying.

## 14. Strategy review triggers

Review this strategy when:

- representative job evidence materially changes role priorities;
- capability evidence supports a stronger or narrower working identity;
- the flagship fails to produce intended career value;
- application or interview feedback shows a repeated pattern;
- geography or work-mode feasibility changes;
- a career cycle ends;
- Ali explicitly requests a strategy review;
- a new primary project is proposed.

## 15. Change standard

A strategy change must state:

- the evidence;
- the career problem being corrected;
- the smallest change;
- what remains fixed;
- affected projects and capability priorities;
- market and portfolio consequences;
- reassessment trigger.

Do not rewrite strategy because of one difficult project session, one exciting technology, or one job description.
